Automatic email to customers

When an order is placed, and again when payment is confirmed, the customer gets an email from your business email, and you get a copy. For Gmail: turn on 2-step verification on the Google account, then Google Account > Security > App passwords, create one and paste the 16 letters below (never your normal Gmail password).

Leave blank to keep the saved one.

Automatic WhatsApp to customers

WhatsApp only allows a website to message customers through the WhatsApp Business Cloud API (a free Meta business account). Steps: 1) create an app at developers.facebook.com and add WhatsApp; 2) note the Phone number ID and create a permanent access token; 3) create a message template (category Utility) named for example order_confirmation with this body: Hello {{1}}, your order {{2}} at Nexus Media Labs is received. Total {{3}}. Status: {{4}}. and wait for Meta to approve it; 4) paste the three values below. In Meta's free test mode only numbers you add as recipients receive messages.

Leave blank to keep the saved one.
